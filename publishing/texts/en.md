# SpaceJumper — English

**Title:** SpaceJumper

**Short description:** Jump higher, collect crystals, and dodge lasers in an endless space arcade game!

**Description:** SpaceJumper is a fast-paced space arcade game where every jump counts. Leap between platforms, collect crystals and energy cores, score points, and beat your personal best. The higher you climb, the tougher it gets: moving platforms, spikes, lasers, and laser mines will test your reflexes. Use shields, a backup module, and short bursts of flight to survive longer. Play on desktop or mobile, with no sign-in required.

**How to play:** On desktop, move with A/D or the left/right arrow keys, jump with Space, and pause with Esc. On mobile, use the on-screen left, right, and jump buttons. Pass through platforms from below and land on top. Collect bonuses and avoid spikes and lasers. While flying, hold Jump to rise.

**Genre:** arcade / platformer. **Language:** English. **Orientation:** portrait gameplay area. **Age rating:** provisional 6+ (confirm in the Console).
